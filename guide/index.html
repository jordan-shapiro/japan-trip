Tokyo · Sat, Dec 26

A morning of eating, Ginza, then Ghibli

Jet lag makes the 4 AM start easier on the first morning. Graze through two markets, see Ginza, nap for about an hour, then out west to Mitaka for the 4:00 Ghibli entry.

  1. 4:15 AM

    Cab to Toyosu

    About 20 minutes.

  2. 5:30 AM

    Tuna auction viewing deck

  3. 6:30 AM

    Sushi breakfast at Toyosu

    Get in line right after the auction. Sushi Dai and Daiwa Sushi are the famous counters; the lines are long, so any counter in the market blocks is a fine backup.

  4. 8:15 AM

    Cab to Tsukiji

    About 10 minutes.

  5. 8:30 AM

    Tsukiji Outer Market, grazing

    Hot tamagoyaki on a stick, beef stew bowls at Kitsuneya, grilled wagyu and scallop skewers, strawberry daifuku. Plenty that isn't raw fish.

  6. 9:45 AM

    Aritsugu knives and Namiyoke shrine

    Knife shop since the 1500s; they engrave names. Then the shrine's giant lion heads.

  7. 10:30 AM

    Tsukiji Hongan-ji

    Indian-style stone temple from 1934, with a pipe organ inside. 5-minute walk.

  8. 11:00 AM

    Kabuki-za, then Itoya

    Theater facade, then 12 floors of stationery.

  9. 12:00 PM

    Ginza 4-chome crossing and Mitsukoshi depachika

    Wako clock tower; the basement food hall for a light lunch. Chuo-dori goes car-free at noon on weekends.

  10. 12:30 PM

    Hakuhinkan Toy Park

    Toy store on many floors, at the south end of Chuo-dori.

  11. 1:00 PM

    Ginza line home from Shimbashi, nap

    Direct, about 20 minutes. Nap until about 2:30.

  12. 2:45 PM

    Leave for Mitaka

    Ginza line to Kanda, JR Chuo Rapid to Mitaka (about 30 minutes), then a 15-minute walk or the yellow community bus.

  13. 4:00 PM

    Ghibli Museum

    All four enter together. No photos inside; the museum closes at 6.

  14. 6:00 PM

    Walk through Inokashira Park to Kichijoji

    About 15 minutes along the pond.

  15. 6:30 PM

    Dinner in Kichijoji

    Harmonica Yokocho, a maze of tiny post-war market alleys. JR Chuo line toward Kanda, then the Ginza line home.

Tokyo · Mon, Dec 28

Old Tokyo morning, river boat, sushi lunch, Shibuya at sunset

Book Shibuya Sky: sunset slots release two weeks ahead at midnight Japan time (Sat Dec 13, 10 AM ET) and sell out fast. Buy adult tickets online; children's tickets are sold only at the counter on the day.

  1. 8:00 AM

    Ueno Park

    A short walk from the hotel. Gold Toshogu shrine, Kiyomizu Kannon-do's "Moon Pine", Hanazono Inari torii tunnel.

  2. 9:00 AM

    Kappabashi Kitchen Street

    Just past the hotel toward Asakusa. Plastic fake-food samples, knives, ceramics, chef's tools. Look for the giant chef head on the Niimi building.

  3. 10:00 AM

    Senso-ji and Nakamise in daylight

    You saw it lit up on the 25th; now the shops are open. Snack on ningyo-yaki cakes and age-manju.

  4. 10:45 AM

    Sumida River water bus, Asakusa to Hama-rikyu Gardens

    From the Azumabashi pier: about 35 minutes under 13 bridges. ¥1,180 adults, ¥470 children, including garden admission. Only about 3 departures a day, so check the Tokyo Cruise timetable. Then a short walk through the garden and on to Daimon. Backup: Toei Asakusa line, Asakusa to Daimon, about 20 minutes.

  5. 12:00 PM

    Sushi Hiroya, omakase lunch

    ¥19,800 per person, paid at the restaurant. Everyone starts together at 12:00, so arrive by 11:50. No perfume or strong fragrance.

  6. 2:15 PM

    Shibuya Parco, 6th floor

    JR Yamanote from Hamamatsucho, about 25 minutes. Nintendo Tokyo, Pokémon Center Shibuya, Capcom Store and the Jump Shop on one floor. Then wander Center Gai.

  7. 4:00 PM

    Shibuya Sky at sunset

    Rooftop deck 229 m above the crossing, in Shibuya Scramble Square right over the station. Sunset is about 4:30. Lockers for bags and hats; the roof is cold and windy.

  8. 5:30 PM

    Shibuya Scramble Crossing

    Walk it at dusk, then watch it from above at the Starbucks or the Magnet by Shibuya109 rooftop. Hachiko statue, the "Myth of Tomorrow" mural, then Mega Don Quijote.

  9. Evening

    Possible dinner: Sushi Ginza Onodera

    An option for tonight. The Ginza line runs direct from Shibuya to Ginza, about 16 minutes. Same day as the Hiroya sushi lunch.

  10. Evening

    Or: Akihabara and a casual dinner on the way home

    Arcades (GiGO, Taito Station) and Radio Kaikan, plus tonkatsu or ramen nearby. Akihabara is one stop from the hotel area.

  11. Late

    Ship big bags to the Kyoto hotel, pack for Hakone

    Hotel luggage delivery or takkyubin. Hakone on overnight bags only.

Kyoto · Sat, Jan 2

Arashiyama, monkeys, Golden Pavilion

Monkey Park is closed Jan 1 and open today. Kinkaku-ji closes at 5 PM, so keep the afternoon moving.

  1. 7:30 AM

    Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and Nonomiya Shrine

    Go early, before the crowds.

  2. 8:45 AM

    Coffee Shop Yamamoto (珈琲店 山本)

    Old-school kissaten in Setogawa-cho, about 10 minutes' walk north of Saga-Arashiyama Station. Order the Yamamoto blend and the beef cutlet sandwich, plus French toast for the kids. Keep it light; lunch is a 9-course temple meal at 12:30. Opens 7 AM, closed Thursdays; check New Year hours.

  3. 10:15 AM

    Tenryu-ji garden

    Come in through the north gate from the bamboo grove path. You need a garden ticket for lunch here anyway.

  4. 12:30 PM

    Lunch at Shigetsu, Tenryu-ji

    Shojin ryori (Buddhist temple vegetarian) on tatami. "Hana" course, ¥9,000 per person, paid at the restaurant, plus the ¥500 Tenryu-ji admission. 90-minute seating.

  5. 1:45 PM

    Iwatayama Monkey Park

    Cross Togetsukyo Bridge. About an hour up and back, a real uphill walk with a big payoff.

  6. 3:30 PM

    Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion

    Taxi, about 20 minutes from Arashiyama. Last entry before 5 PM.

  7. If time

    Ryoan-ji rock garden

    A 15-minute walk from Kinkaku-ji, also closes at 5.

  8. 5:15 PM

    Taxi to Gion

    About 25–30 minutes from Kinkaku-ji/Ryoan-ji to Shijo-Ohashi. Department store lucky-bag (fukubukuro) sales are on today too, if anyone wants a detour.

  9. 5:45 PM

    Soba: Matsuba, nishin soba

    The shop that invented nishin soba (sweet-simmered herring on soba) in 1882, at the east end of Shijo Bridge next to Minamiza. Nishin soba about ¥1,650; plain kake soba or tempura soba for the kids. Normally 10:30 AM–9 PM (last order 8:30), closed Wednesdays; check New Year hours.

  10. 6:45 PM

    Gyoza: Gion Gyoza Hohei

    5-minute walk into Gion. Tiny Tabelog 100 counter making small, very garlicky one-bite gyoza (garlic-free and ginger versions too). Cheap, walk-in only, expect a line. Usually opens 5 or 6 PM, closed Sundays; check New Year hours. Backup: Chao Chao Gyoza, Sanjo-Kiyamachi, 10 minutes across the river (opens 2 PM on Saturdays).
