Tokyo · Sun, Dec 27

Harajuku, two big meals, teamLab

Early start: be at Meiji Jingu's main hall for the 8:00 AM morning offering, then coffee and Harajuku.

  1. ⛩️
    7:40 AM

    Meiji Jingu (Shinto shrine to Emperor Meiji): 8:00 AM morning offering

    Head out by about 7:00. From the Harajuku torii it's a 10-minute walk on the gravel path past the sake barrels to the main hall; aim to be in the courtyard by 7:50.

    8:00 AM: Asamike, the morning offering (part of the daily Nikku-sai). Taiko drums announce it, then priests present food offerings to the kami and pray for peace, about 10 minutes. Watch quietly from the courtyard and don't photograph the priests. It happens every day at 8 AM and 2 PM; there is no changing of the guard.

    After: The paired "married" camphor trees, write an ema, and draw an omikokoro (Meiji Jingu gives poems by the Emperor and Empress instead of fortunes). Sunday mornings often have Shinto wedding processions, mostly from about 10 AM.

    Nearby coffee and shops
