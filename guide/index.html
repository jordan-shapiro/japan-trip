Tokyo · Sat, Dec 26

A morning of eating, Ginza, then Ghibli

Jet lag makes the 4 AM start easier on the first morning. Graze through two markets, see Ginza, nap for about an hour, then out west to Mitaka for the 4:00 Ghibli entry.

  1. 4:15 AM

    Cab to Toyosu

    About 20 minutes.

  2. 5:30 AM

    Tuna auction viewing deck

  3. 6:30 AM

    Sushi breakfast at Toyosu

    Get in line right after the auction. Sushi Dai and Daiwa Sushi are the famous counters; the lines are long, so any counter in the market blocks is a fine backup.

  4. 8:15 AM

    Cab to Tsukiji

    About 10 minutes.

  5. 8:30 AM

    Tsukiji Outer Market, grazing

    Hot tamagoyaki on a stick, beef stew bowls at Kitsuneya, grilled wagyu and scallop skewers, strawberry daifuku. Plenty that isn't raw fish.

  6. 9:45 AM

    Aritsugu Tokyo knives, then Namiyoke Shrine

    Aritsugu, 4-13-6 Tsukiji: knifemakers since 1560, open 6 AM–3 PM (closed Sundays and Wednesdays). They help you choose, hand-engrave names free and will assemble a knife while you wait (about 30 minutes). Then Namiyoke Shrine's giant lion heads, around the corner.

  7. 10:30 AM

    Tsukiji Hongan-ji

    Indian-style stone temple from 1934, with a pipe organ inside. 5-minute walk.

  8. 11:00 AM

    Kabuki-za, then Itoya

    Theater facade, then 12 floors of stationery. Keep it to about 30 minutes.

  9. 11:30 AM

    Tonkatsu lunch at Ginza Bairin

    Ginza's first tonkatsu shop, open since 1927, and the inventor of the katsu sando and its own tonkatsu sauce. After the morning's grazing, share a rosu katsu set, a katsudon and a katsu sando. Basement of the Bairin building, 7-8-1 Ginza; walk-in. Open daily 11:30 AM–9 PM. On the way, the Ginza 4-chome crossing and Wako clock tower; Chuo-dori goes car-free at noon on weekends.

  10. 12:00 PM

    Hakuhinkan Toy Park

    Toy store on many floors, at the south end of Chuo-dori.

  11. 12:30 PM

    Ginza line home from Shimbashi, nap

    Direct, about 20 minutes. Nap until about 1:45.

  12. 2:00 PM

    Leave for Kichijoji

    Ginza line to Kanda, then JR Chuo Rapid to Kichijoji. About 45 minutes door to door.

  13. 2:50 PM

    Inokashira Park in daylight

    Park entrance a few minutes south of Kichijoji Station. Walk down to the pond, see the red Inokashira Benzaiten shrine on the water, then follow the park paths west to the museum (about 15 minutes). Sunset is around 4:30, so this is the last good light.

  14. 4:00 PM

    Ghibli Museum

    All four enter together. No photos inside; the museum closes at 6.

  15. 6:00 PM

    Back to Kichijoji

    About 15 minutes on foot. The park is dark by now, so take the streets, or the yellow community bus.

  16. 6:20 PM

    Snack: Satou menchi-katsu

    1-1-8 Kichijoji Honcho, at the entrance to Harmonica Yokocho. Famous fried minced-beef cutlets, eaten hot from the paper; the line moves fast. Open until 7:30 PM. One each is plenty before dinner.

  17. 6:30 PM

    Dinner in Kichijoji

    Harmonica Yokocho, a maze of tiny post-war market alleys. JR Chuo line toward Kanda, then the Ginza line home.

Tokyo · Mon, Dec 28

Old Tokyo morning, river boat, sushi lunch, Shibuya at sunset

Book Shibuya Sky: sunset slots release two weeks ahead at midnight Japan time (Sat Dec 13, 10 AM ET) and sell out fast. Buy adult tickets online; children's tickets are sold only at the counter on the day.

  1. 8:00 AM

    Ueno Park

    A short walk from the hotel. Gold Toshogu shrine, Kiyomizu Kannon-do's "Moon Pine", Hanazono Inari torii tunnel.

  2. 9:00 AM

    Senso-ji and Nakamise in daylight

    Short cab from Ueno. You saw it lit up on the 25th; now the shops are open. Snack on ningyo-yaki cakes and age-manju.

  3. 10:00 AM

    Kappabashi: specific shops

    10-minute walk west of Senso-ji. Kamata Hakensha, 2-12-6 Matsugaya: knives, free engraving, English-speaking staff, opens 10 AM. Ganso Food Sample Shop, 3-7-6 Nishi-Asakusa: the most realistic fake food (taiyaki keychains, sushi magnets), opens 10 AM. Kama-Asa, 2-24-1 Matsugaya, opens at 11, too late for this morning. The giant chef head is on the Niimi building at the south end. Then walk back to the Azumabashi pier by Asakusa station, about 15 minutes.

  4. 10:45 AM

    Sumida River water bus, Asakusa to Hama-rikyu Gardens

    From the Azumabashi pier: about 35 minutes under 13 bridges. ¥1,180 adults, ¥470 children, including garden admission. Only about 3 departures a day, so check the Tokyo Cruise timetable. Then a short walk through the garden and on to Daimon. Backup: Toei Asakusa line, Asakusa to Daimon, about 20 minutes.

  5. 12:00 PM

    Sushi Hiroya, omakase lunch

    ¥19,800 per person, paid at the restaurant. Everyone starts together at 12:00, so arrive by 11:50. No perfume or strong fragrance.

  6. 2:15 PM

    Shibuya Parco, 6th floor

    JR Yamanote from Hamamatsucho, about 25 minutes. Nintendo Tokyo, Pokémon Center Shibuya, Capcom Store and the Jump Shop on one floor. Then wander Center Gai.

  7. 4:00 PM

    Shibuya Sky at sunset

    Rooftop deck 229 m above the crossing, in Shibuya Scramble Square right over the station. Sunset is about 4:30. Lockers for bags and hats; the roof is cold and windy.

  8. 5:30 PM

    Shibuya Scramble Crossing

    Walk it at dusk, then watch it from above at the Starbucks or the Magnet by Shibuya109 rooftop. Hachiko statue, the "Myth of Tomorrow" mural, then Mega Don Quijote.

  9. 6:15 PM

    Arcade and karaoke in Shibuya

    Taito Station Shibuya, 1-24-12 Shibuya (Toei Plaza), open until midnight: crane, rhythm and racing games. Then an hour of karaoke at Big Echo Shibuya Center-gai Honten, 21-8 Udagawacho, open 24 hours, with English song search. Ask for a non-smoking room and the total price including any room fee before you go in. Kids are fine with you before 11 PM.

  10. Evening

    Possible dinner: Sushi Ginza Onodera

    An option for tonight. The Ginza line runs direct from Shibuya to Ginza, about 16 minutes. Same day as the Hiroya sushi lunch.

  11. Evening

    Super Potato, 3–5F, 1-11-2 Sotokanda (retro games, tiny arcade upstairs); Akihabara Gachapon Hall, 3-15-5 Sotokanda (hundreds of capsule machines); both open 11 AM–8 PM. Then GiGO arcades and Radio Kaikan, plus tonkatsu or ramen nearby. One stop from the hotel area.

Hakone · Wed, Dec 30

The Hakone loop, lake end first, then Kyoto by lantern

Do the loop backwards from the usual Tokyo direction: the ryokan is on the lake side, so start at Lake Ashi and finish at Hakone-Yumoto and Odawara, where the bullet train is. Everything runs both ways and the Freepass covers it all. Ropeway hours in December are 9:00 to 4:15; check Hakone Navi that morning for wind closures (a replacement bus runs). Peak New Year travel day, so reserved Hikari seats are essential.

  1. Early

    Optional: the old Tokaido stone road near Ashinoyu.

  2. 8:30 AM

  3. 9:00 AM

  12. 5:45 PM

  13. 8:00 PM

Kyoto · Thu, Dec 31

New Year's Eve: Fushimi Inari at dawn

The last quiet morning before the New Year shrine crowds. Fushimi Inari is about 6 minutes on the Keihan from Kiyomizu-Gojo.

  1. 6:45 AM

    Fushimi Inari

    Sunrise is around 7:05. Full loop to the summit; the Yotsutsuji viewpoint is the halfway payoff. Down by about 9:30.

  2. Late AM

    Back to the apartment, rest

  3. 12:30 PM

    Soba lunch at Honke Owariya

    Kyoto's oldest soba house, founded in 1465, in a Meiji-era townhouse near Karasuma-Oike. New Year's Eve is toshikoshi soba day, so it's the year's busiest; expect a line. Order the Horai soba (stacked lacquer boxes with toppings) or a simple zaru soba. Open 11 AM; closed Jan 1–2. Walk to Nishiki after, about 15 minutes.

  4. Afternoon

    Nishiki Market, and the original Aritsugu

    Aritsugu Kyoto, 219 Kajiya-cho at the market's east end: the original shop of the knifemakers you saw in Tsukiji, open 10 AM–5 PM, closed Wednesdays. If you want one great knife, buy it here or at Tsukiji, not both. The market is mobbed today, its biggest New Year shopping day; Jan 1–3 it's mostly shut.

  5. Afternoon

    Matcha at Tsujiri on Shijo

  6. 7:00 PM

    Kodaiji Tantra, New Year tempura kaiseki

    ¥36,300 per person, paid at the restaurant; 3-hour seating.

  7. 7:30 PM

    Tempura Endo Yasaka, counter

    Prepaid ¥126,560 for 4. Its rules require a private room for parties with a child under 13.

  8. 10:30 PM

    Okera mairi at Yasaka Shrine

    Carry the sacred fire home on a rope. Or joya no kane, the 108 bell strikes at Chion-in. Both are a short walk from dinner. Walk home; taxis won't exist.

Kyoto · Sat, Jan 2

Arashiyama, monkeys, Golden Pavilion

Monkey Park is closed Jan 1 and open today. Kinkaku-ji closes at 5 PM, so keep the afternoon moving.

  1. 7:30 AM

    Go early, before the crowds.

  2. 8:45 AM

  4. 12:30 PM

  5. 1:45 PM

    Iwatayama Monkey Park

    Cross Togetsukyo Bridge. About an hour up and back, a real uphill walk with a big payoff.

  6. 3:30 PM

    Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion

    Taxi, about 20 minutes from Arashiyama. Last entry before 5 PM.

  7. If time

    Ryoan-ji rock garden

    A 15-minute walk from Kinkaku-ji, also closes at 5.

  8. 5:15 PM

  9. 5:45 PM

    Michelin Bib Gourmand bowl on Teramachi near Bukkoji: a clear dashi-shoyu broth (white or black shoyu). Cash only. Reopens Jan 2 after its New Year break. Then a 12-minute walk across Shijo Bridge to Gion.

  10. 6:45 PM
